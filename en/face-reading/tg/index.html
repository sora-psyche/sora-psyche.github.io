    Background Guide

    Eyebrow Reading: Shapes and the Age-30 Mark

    In physiognomy, the eyebrows are considered a person’s “running headline”: they show how you communicate and take action — and often what unfolds around your siblings.

    Full, dense eyebrows

    The denser the eyebrows, the more strongly you seek exchange and connection with others. What you begin, you usually carry forward steadily.

    Eyebrows with changing density

    Dense near the bridge of the nose and lighter from the middle outward: you launch projects with clarity and drive, but need fresh motivation later to stay the course.

    Thin, fine eyebrows

    The finer the eyebrows, the more selective you are. You do not grant trust immediately — not out of harshness, but out of caution.

    Thin, irregular eyebrows

    Fine, uneven brows point to a sensitive, shifting mood. You are selective and sometimes react on impulse.

    Eyebrows widening toward the tail

    You take a little time to warm up to new situations — yet over time you become increasingly steady and reliable.

    Low-set eyebrows over the eye

    Low-set brows show a realist: you look closely and prefer to prepare for challenges in advance.

    High-set angled eyebrows

    High brows show openness to the world. A distinct angle stands for self-confidence and temperament.

    The Age-30 Mark

    When a gap or break is visible:

    In the center of your eyebrow there is a small break or a sparser spot. In physiognomy, this indicates that around age 30 (sometimes a little earlier, around 27) there was or will be a turning point that gives your life a new direction.

    When no break is visible:

    In the center of your eyebrow — the area associated with roughly age 30 — the hair flow is even and without a noticeable interruption.
